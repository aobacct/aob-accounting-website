About AOB Accounting LLC

Built on clarity, care, and technical depth.

AOB Accounting was built on the belief that financial clarity creates stability, opportunity, and peace of mind.

Founder Alejandra Ortiz Briseño knew from a young age accounting was her calling and has spent over a decade intentionally building the knowledge and experience to serve small businesses and individuals with care and integrity.

Before launching AOB Accounting, Alejandra worked at CPA firms, including a Top 20 firm, supporting high-net-worth and ultra-high-net-worth individuals. Earlier in her career, she also worked with small businesses through smaller CPA firms, building hands-on experience in bookkeeping, payroll, compliance, and day-to-day accounting operations.

At the heart of the firm is a strong commitment to community, especially the Hispanic community. AOB Accounting strives to be a trusted, approachable partner that brings clarity to the numbers and helps clients make confident decisions for long-term growth.

Prepare

What to bring to the consultation.

Clients do not need everything perfectly organized. Bring what is available, and we can discuss what is missing during the consultation.

Accounting Records

  • Bank statements, if available
  • QuickBooks access or reports, if applicable
  • Previous individual income tax returns
  • Monthly, quarterly, or annual excise tax returns, such as WA DOR reports
  • Payroll: copies of the most recent annual and quarterly payroll tax returns, if applicable
    • Federal
      • Forms W-3, W-2, 940, and 941
    • State
      • ESD
      • L&I
      • PFML

Questions and Goals

  • What do you need help with now?
  • Are there any notices or deadlines?
  • What are your goals for your business or finances?
